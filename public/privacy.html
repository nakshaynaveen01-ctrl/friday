Friday Assistant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Friday Assistant is a private, personal assistant operated by one individual for their own use. It is not offered to the public and only replies to phone numbers its owner has explicitly allowed.

What is processed

When an allowed number sends a WhatsApp message to Friday Assistant, the message text and the sender's phone number are received through the WhatsApp Business Platform so that a reply can be generated and sent back.

How it is used

Message text is sent to an AI model provider (Google Gemini) to produce a reply. With the owner's own connected accounts, the assistant may also read the owner's email and calendar to answer the owner's questions. Messages are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared with anyone else.

Storage

Only the last few messages of a conversation are kept, in temporary memory, so that replies stay in context. Reminders and notes the owner asks the assistant to keep are stored on the assistant's server. Messages from numbers that are not on the allowed list are ignored and not stored.

Deleting data

The owner can delete stored notes and reminders at any time. Anyone else who believes their data was processed can ask for it to be deleted by contacting the app owner through the contact details listed for this app on Meta.
